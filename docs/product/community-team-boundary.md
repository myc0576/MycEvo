# MycEvo Community and Team Boundary

## Principle

The public single-user product must solve a complete local workflow-governance problem. Future Team revenue begins with multi-person coordination, not with locking a user's own data or weakening local trust.

## Public single-user target

- local workspace and portable formats;
- capture, closeout and candidate proposal;
- evidence, diff and provenance;
- explicit human approve/reject/defer;
- canonical lineage and rollback;
- cross-Agent handoff;
- import, export, deletion and migration;
- CLI/MCP, public adapters and public packs;
- security fixes and compatible readers.

The current technical preview does not yet ship every target row. Exact status is maintained in `docs/release/community-release-contract.md`.

## Future Team

- organization, member, invitation and role management;
- RBAC and administration;
- shared canonical state;
- review queues and multi-person approval policies;
- hosted or encrypted synchronization;
- concurrent-change conflict handling and notifications;
- team history, audit and policy distribution;
- private packs, connectors and templates;
- backup, retention and recovery.

## Future Enterprise

- private deployment;
- SSO/SAML/SCIM;
- compliance retention and log export;
- enterprise security controls;
- SLA, support and commercial embedding rights.

## Permanent portability guarantees

The following do not belong behind a mandatory Team paywall:

- export and deletion of user-owned data;
- single-user provenance and audit;
- human promotion authority;
- security and compatibility fixes;
- reading/migrating published public formats.

No pricing decision is part of this boundary document.
