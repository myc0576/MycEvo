# MycEvo Trademark Policy — Draft

`MycEvo` and its official visual identity identify the official project and services.

You may truthfully describe compatibility, for example “works with MycEvo,” provided that the description does not imply sponsorship or official status.

Without written permission, do not:

- present a modified distribution as the official MycEvo release;
- use MycEvo branding for a white-label or competing hosted service;
- use confusingly similar names or logos that imply endorsement.

This draft requires owner review before public release.
