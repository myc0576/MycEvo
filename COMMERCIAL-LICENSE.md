# MycEvo Commercial Licensing — Draft Boundary

This document is an informational draft, not a commercial offer and not a substitute for the repository license.

The intended source-available license permits internal business use and personal/non-commercial use. A separate written commercial license is expected for use cases such as:

- offering MycEvo itself as a paid hosted or multi-tenant service;
- white-labeling MycEvo as another vendor's product;
- reselling direct access to substantially the same MycEvo software;
- embedding MycEvo where the applicable source-available license does not grant the required rights.

No price, plan or payment process is defined yet. Internal use by a technical, research or startup team is intentionally distinct from selling MycEvo itself as a service.

The final boundary is controlled by the approved `LICENSE.md` and a signed commercial agreement. This file must not add restrictions that are absent from those documents.
